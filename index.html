Proven Treatment Model

Specialized Care Built on Exposure & Response Prevention.

Standard talk therapy often falls short for OCD and severe anxiety because discussing fears can inadvertently feed the anxiety loop. Our licensed specialists use Exposure and Response Prevention (ERP)—the most clinically proven behavioral therapy in the world.

Targeted Exposure Sessions

Systematically desensitize anxiety triggers in a supportive, step-by-step clinical setting.

Compulsion & Ritual Elimination

Rewire the brain's alarm system by eliminating checking, washing, reassurance seeking, and avoidance.

Family Accommodation Coaching

Empower parents and partners with concrete tools to support recovery without reinforcing fear.

Individualized Levels of Care

Programs Tailored to Your Recovery Needs

We provide structured outpatient levels of care designed to fit into school, work, and family schedules.

Intensive Care 3-5 Days / Week

Intensive Outpatient Program (IOP)

Designed for moderate-to-severe OCD, debilitating panic, or acute school refusal. Provides intensive daily exposure therapy with rapid symptom reduction.

  • Daily 1-on-1 clinician exposures
  • Peer cohort support & practice
  • Weekly family coaching sessions
Weekly Therapy 1-2 Days / Week

General Outpatient Therapy (OP)

Weekly individual ERP and CBT sessions for patients transitioning from intensive care or managing mild-to-moderate anxiety without major life disruption.

  • 50-minute individual sessions
  • Real-world behavioral homework
  • Relapse prevention protocols
Pediatric & Teen Ages 6 to 17

Child & Adolescent Programs

Specialized pediatric interventions for youth battling separation anxiety, school avoidance, bedtime rituals, and contamination or scrupulosity OCD.

  • Age-appropriate gamified exposures
  • Parent accommodation reduction
  • 504 / IEP school coordination

Confidential Clinical Screener

Do You or a Loved One Need Specialized Care?

Answer 3 brief questions to determine whether Outpatient or Intensive Outpatient (IOP) is right for your symptoms.

1. How much time each day is consumed by intrusive thoughts, worries, or rituals?
2. To what degree do avoidance or compulsions impact school, work, or relationships?
3. Have you tried traditional talk therapy without adequate symptom relief?

Recommended Care Pathway
